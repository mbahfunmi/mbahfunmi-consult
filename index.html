About Me

Systems builder.
Real life thinker.

I'm Mbah Funmi — a Notion consultant and systems designer. I got into workspace design because I believe the best systems aren't just beautiful — they work on a Tuesday morning when life is messy.

My background in front-end web development, Salesforce administration, and AI-powered workflow design means I bring technical precision and systems thinking to every workspace I build.

Right now, you exist in two worlds. The physical world you were born into — and the virtual world you move through every day. The physical has borders. The virtual doesn't. It is the most abundant, most accessible, and most underused space available today— and it belongs to those who build in it. Amazon built there. Creators built there. Now founders and small businesses are building there. Notion is the foundation I use to help you claim your space in it — designed, structured, and AI-powered so your business doesn't just survive in the virtual world. It thrives.
